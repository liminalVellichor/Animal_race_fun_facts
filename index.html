        About these numbers

        These are peak burst speeds: the fastest each animal has been recorded or reliably estimated to go, usually over a short distance. Figures vary between sources and between individuals, so treat them as approximate. Sky speeds are for flight and water speeds for swimming, and the peregrine falcon’s figure is for a dive. Speeds marked disputed rest on contested measurements.

        Race times assume every animal hits top speed at the start and holds it to the finish. No real animal does that, which is why endurance runners such as the pronghorn, wolf and human do far better over long distances than this page suggests.
